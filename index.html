About Dr. Phan

31 years of
surgical excellence

Dr. Tâm Phan is a DMD and Fellow of both the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI) and the Misch International Implant Institute (FMIII). A graduate with honors from Laval University's Faculty of Dentistry, he has dedicated over 31 years to mastering aesthetic dentistry, implantology, and full mouth rehabilitation — trained under some of the world's most respected implantology pioneers.
