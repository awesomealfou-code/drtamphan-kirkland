Founder & Lead Surgeon

Dr. Tâm Phan

Dental Surgeon · Implantologist

Dr. Tâm Phan is a board-certified dental surgeon with over 20 years of experience transforming smiles and restoring oral health across four continents. Renowned for his meticulous surgical precision and artist's eye for natural aesthetics, he has performed over 2,500 implant procedures with a 98% long-term success rate.

A sought-after international speaker, Dr. Phan has presented at over 40 conferences worldwide and serves as a clinical trainer for the next generation of implantologists.

D.M.D., Université de Montréal, Faculty of Dental Medicine
Fellow, International Team for Implantology (ITI)
Certified in Advanced Oral Surgery & Implantology
Specialist in Facial Aesthetics — Botox, PRGF & Masseter Therapy
